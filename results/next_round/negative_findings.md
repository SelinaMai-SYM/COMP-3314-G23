# Negative and conditional findings

The practical four-benchmark +2pp ACC/nondecreasing NMI target is **not established**. Formal averages are NA. No complete fair 15-configuration capped search is claimed.

- The locked incorrect-fusion gate supports 0/4 datasets, below the required two. Real-data C is not triggered. This does not rule out incorrect fusion at other gamma values.
- Oracle correction helps at some large gamma values, but is label-assisted and is compared separately with same-gamma matched controls. It is not a usable unsupervised method.
- At gamma=1, small shrinkage gains do not become stable net benefit across datasets. USPS remains an optimization limitation at important gamma values.
- Historical USPS E8/Y0/greedy gamma=1000 ACC 79.918/NMI 85.609 remains a strong recorded point. Fresh seeded F/W differ or lack provable historical identity; their uncertified poor outputs cannot invalidate that point or prove an objective limitation.
- Synthetic capped fusion avoids bridge-edge damage but matches simpler static deletion and squared fidelity; SC already separates those examples. At anchor contamination it can remove useful correction.
- The five-point local capped solution depends strongly on initialization (objectives 3 versus 8). Only exhaustive tiny-state enumeration supplies a global reference.
- The N1 primary synthetic pilot changes MSE without ACC/NMI improvement. No real-data superiority is established.
- Projected heat improves graph stability in the geometry pilot; primary clustering outcomes and hard-dimension/W-only controls are in heat_primary_summary.csv. Stability is not label accuracy. Restricted exact-kernel approximation diagnostics do not imply full-graph kNN fidelity.
- Corrected image robustness uses four fixed conditions, full reruns and native domains. These are descriptive conditions, not independent perturbation replicates; USPS uncertainty remains.
- Native prclust is validated only on a tiny software sanity check; external RCC is source-inspected, not a benchmark comparison.
- Old missing F/W/G arrays, unavailable historical dataset identities remain explicit limitations.

All retained failures, cold and warm solver versions, trials, costs and unexecuted planned cells are published.
