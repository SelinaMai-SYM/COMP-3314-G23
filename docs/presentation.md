# Group 23 presentation outline

Nine minutes of presentation are followed by three minutes of questions. All
four authors participate: Han Jiale, Lu Sinan, Mahatharadol Vie and Mai Sheung Ying.

## 1. Problem and two graphs — 0:00–1:00

Introduce Spectral Shrunk Clustering, the input graph A0, its spectral F0 and the
separate shrinkage graph W0. Explain the distinction between an optimized
representation and improved class assignments.

## 2. Paper evidence and reproduction — 1:00–2:30

Show one coupled ACC/NMI comparison and its source/solver qualifications.
Explain the 72-cell inventory, source adaptations and retrospective subset
matching. Distinguish reported paper values from our measurements.

## 3. What the diagnostics support — 2:30–3:30

Use the retained numerical and fixed-graph evidence. Normalization, oracle
interventions and shrinkage are different effects. Explain why a solved
objective, motion or connectivity alone cannot establish a clustering gain.

## 4. Relative stopping distances — 3:30–5:00

Present the frozen tolerance and the global dispersion bound. Show measured
ACC/NMI against ordinary SSC and interpolation when available; otherwise display
the exact numerical status. The bound does not guarantee class separation.

## 5. Tangent constraints — 5:00–6:15

Show the predeclared radial-neutralization gate and both development outcomes.
If promoted, distinguish tangency from normalization and an isotropic movement
ball. A failed gate is shown as such, without substituted benchmark scores.

## 6. Frozen relative neighbors — 6:15–7:30

Explain label-blind comparisons and development-only semantic screening. If
promoted, compare graph coupling with triplet-only and matched random forces.
Present coverage, frozen-reference and normalization limitations.

## 7. Evidence and lessons — 7:30–9:00

Use the final eight-benchmark status/metric table. State what passed numerical
verification, which mechanism is supported, and what simple control explains
any gain. Retain neutral and negative findings and incomplete qualified coverage.

## Discussion material

Keep derivations, certificates, source versions, full controls and conditional
intervals in the report appendix. Explain convexity/duality, graph spectra and
unsupervised fitting without claiming unseen-data evaluation or global novelty.
