# MSRA50

Status: `unavailable`

Chang et al., AAAI 2015, state 1799 images, 12 classes, resized to 32×32, and cite no source. He, Yan, Hu, Niyogi, and Zhang, TPAMI 2005, describe a Microsoft Research Asia collection of 12 people; that paper does not state a total of 1799 and does not give a download URL.

No archive with those measured counts was downloaded on 2026-10-20. Unofficial CSDN `.mat` bundles, MSRA-B, MSRA10K, MSRA-CFW, and MSRA-TD500 were not substituted.

`load_dataset` raises `DatasetUnavailable`. See `manifest.yaml`.
