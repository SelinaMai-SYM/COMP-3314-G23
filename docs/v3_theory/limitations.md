# Limitations

- The independent ADMM check often stops with a worse exact objective than IRLS on real graphs. Those runs stay `NUMERICALLY_UNCERTIFIED`. A small change in `G` is not treated as a proof.
- Seed-level intervals do not account for uncertainty in `F`, `W`, or the data sample. The dataset-level sample has four units.
- YaleB Y3 is a different input. It is not evidence that an extension closed the historical gap.
- Mass normalization is algebraically a gamma rescaling. A match to the equivalent-gamma control is a negative result for "new method" language.
- Stability is the frequency of an edge under brightness and contrast changes. It is not same-identity accuracy.
- UMIST 575 and the BinAlpha candidate are supplemental. AR 840, UMIST 564, MSRA50, and LDA-SC remain blocked.
- No claim is made that X1--X5 are novel.
- Orders above 4000 solve the same symmetrized IRLS system with conjugate gradients. The two USPS random-graph controls used that path after SuperLU fill-in stalled. Completed local-graph jobs kept their SuperLU solutions.
- `eigsh` is unseeded. The assembled UMIST block uses one cached embedding. An earlier embedding is stored under `results/v3_theory/superseded/` and is excluded from the primary tables.
- JAFFE X4 and X5, dengcai Y0 mass-tau, and both YaleB Y3 alpha=1 solves missed the iteration limit. They are exploratory and were left out of the seed-paired rows.
- The YaleB Laplacian diagnostic records large eigenpair residuals. Those residuals qualify the spectrum table. They are not a second clustering experiment.
- Branch deletion and the independent-root migration wait for approval. The candidate branch was not part of the frozen experiment.
