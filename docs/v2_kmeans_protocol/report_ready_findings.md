# Investigating the Role of K-means Initialization in SSC Reproduction

Chang et al. (AAAI 2015) report means and standard deviations, and they also refer to repeated runs chosen by the clustering objective. Those statements do not identify today's scikit-learn defaults. We therefore held the reconstructed E8 representations fixed and compared four aggregations. P1 is the mean of 50 random Lloyd runs, seeds 0 through 49, `n_init=1`. P2 is the single minimum-inertia run inside that same batch. P3 and P4 are the mean and the minimum-inertia run of one batch of scikit-learn greedy k-means++ trials. Labels were used only to score a finished partition, with Hungarian accuracy and geometric-mean NMI. SC clusters the spectral embedding F. SSC clusters the shrunk patterns G. The two sides share the graph, the embedding, and the K-means rule.

At gamma 1, greedy k-means++ accounts for most of the random-Lloyd deficit on ORL and JAFFE, but it does so for SC and SSC together. ORL moves from about 57 to about 78 on both representations. JAFFE moves from about 68 to about 95 on both. The published SSC-over-SC gains, +3.3 on ORL and +5.1 on JAFFE, are not recovered. Minimum-inertia selection has the same character: it can raise accuracy, especially on JAFFE, and the selected SSC partition is not better than the selected SC partition. One selected run is not a mean plus or minus a standard deviation. Across ten independent best-of-50 batches, that conclusion is stable for greedy k-means++ and still short of the paper for random minimum-inertia on ORL.

YaleB remains the clearest negative result. On `cropped2414`, every protocol stays near 18–22 percent accuracy, against published SC 34.8 and SSC 49.8. On `dengcai32` the best primary cell is about 25.6. The two matrices were not averaged. The deficit is already present in F, so the initializer is not the YaleB explanation. USPS at gamma 1, where the solver did converge, likewise shows almost no shrinkage gain (SSC about 61.7–65.8 against a published 75.5). On the prespecified gamma grid, USPS at `1e3` raises SSC P1 to 73.86 while SC stays near 61.7, but that solve did not converge and selecting this gamma by accuracy would be an oracle. BinAlpha and the 575-image UMIST file are non-exact and are not paper comparisons. AR and MSRA50 were blocked rather than filled with zeros.

Under this reconstruction, the final K-means protocol explains the large random-initialization gap on ORL and JAFFE, and the shrinkage stage at gamma 1 contributes little additional accuracy. It does not explain the published SC-to-SSC gains, and it does not explain YaleB. K-means++ is not proposed as a new method. The authors' initializer, gamma schedule, and image files remain unidentified.
